In 2026, five strangers are invited to an exclusive, high-stakes poker game at a remote, high-tech estate owned by the enigmatic Samuel Nicholas (Seth Green). The invitees include Michael Quinn (Kevin Dillon), a detective with mounting gambling debts; Billy Gray (Charlie Weber), a professional fixer; Tom Cartright (Dylan Walsh), a disgraced corporate executive; Aurora (Eloise Lovell Anderson), a socialite; and Dr. Scott Stevens (Dan Bucatinsky), a surgeon. Upon arrival, the group is stripped of their communication devices and led to a subterranean bunker designed as a luxury gaming suite.

As the first hand begins, Nicholas reveals that the buy-in was not the money they brought, but rather "moral equity." He demonstrates that the table is integrated with a sophisticated surveillance system that has unmasked their most guarded secrets. Nicholas explains that the game is "total loss": the loser of each round will not only be eliminated from the table but will have their personal and professional crimes broadcasted to the global authorities and the public in real-time. The tension escalates when Dr. Stevens loses the first round; as he is escorted out, a live news feed on the wall monitors shows his medical malpractice records being leaked, leading to his immediate televised arrest.

The psychological warfare intensifies as the remaining players realize the game is rigged to force them into betraying one another. Quinn and Gray form a tentative alliance to identify Nicholas’s weakness, but the host reveals that he is actually the brother of a woman who died due to the collective negligence of all five players during a multi-vehicle hit-and-run years prior. The "game" is a calculated act of retribution.

As the "blinds" increase, so does the physical danger; the room's life-support systems begin to fail for those with the lowest chip counts. In the final round, only Quinn and Celeste (Chloe Fox)—a late arrival who was revealed to be an undercover accomplice of Nicholas—remain. However, Celeste turns on Nicholas, realizing his plan involves no survivors. In a violent confrontation, Quinn uses his tactical knowledge to disable the estate's security grid. Nicholas is killed in the ensuing struggle as the bunker begins to self-destruct. Quinn and Celeste escape the estate just as the police arrive, though they are left with the realization that their secrets are still scheduled for release. The film ends with Quinn watching his own arrest warrant flash on a nearby screen.
